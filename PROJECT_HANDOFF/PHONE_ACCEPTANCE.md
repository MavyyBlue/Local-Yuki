# Galaxy S26+ acceptance

Use matching-signature CI APKs. Preserve a password-protected Vault first; keep its password separately. Install over the existing apps without uninstall/reset. Android upgrade must preserve identity, memories, settings and previous evidence.

1. Check the delivered source identity against the CI “exact tested commit” and unchanged signing certificate. Launch Local Yuki and Lockdown; confirm migration retained existing data. Open Inner life/Memory/State and inspect old history.
2. With a cool phone and visible Local Yuki, import a compatible small GGUF. Inspect architecture/quantization/size/KV and measured admission receipt. Admit System One, System Two and Language (one model may fill all); admit a compatible embedding model. Exercise disable/replacement/unload/remove. Malformed, incompatible, hash-changed and oversized files must be refused without process/device instability. A model that executes but produces poor behavior should be disabled/replaced.
3. Greet Yuki, have a multi-turn conversation, remember a fact, ask semantically related wording, pose an ambiguous question, and ask for an opinion. Supply new evidence and inspect retained opinion/history. Ask what she considered while away; responses must correspond to durable reflection records. Read displayed uncertainty and partial-recall warnings.
4. Grant only intended capabilities using Autonomy setup and actual Android settings. Verify status, structured screen/notifications, offline speech/TTS, visible wake phrase, document search and local image/screenshot text. Revoke a grant and repeat: fail truthfully. Password/authority screens and unsupported general vision must not be represented as perceived/control success.
5. In Lockdown enable its explicit Local Yuki control toggle. From Local Yuki inspect state and exercise protected apps/domains/plans, limits/schedules/modes, companion enablement/size/position/wander/reduced motion, dialogue/reaction, and break controls. Verify real resulting Lockdown state and its minimum bypass countdown. Disable the toggle: IPC must fail. Use your existing protections, not invented UI success.
6. Enable initiative with quiet hours/cooldown and a small daily limit. Add a pending concern and an owner-requested deferred note/action; inspect its stored plan. Leave the app, receive at most policy-allowed grounded initiative, and return to inspect reflection/delivery/action records. Quiet hours/revoked grants must suppress/block relevant actions. JobScheduler may delay work under Doze; force-stop intentionally stops scheduling.
7. Use TikTok/video/gaming/camera or multitasking, then summon Yuki. Observe constrained profile when heat/RAM/battery conditions warrant, bounded inference and unload after each pass. Stop must promptly cancel; backgrounding, low-memory and power-save must defer/cancel optional cognition. Do not intentionally overheat the device.
8. Reopen after ordinary process death and after a reboot. Identity, concerns, history, cooldown and scheduled plans must persist. Interrupted external actions should show unknown/review status, not be silently repeated. Verify sleep/resume recovery.
9. Export a Vault, restore into a fresh test installation/database or later another owner phone, and compare identity/personality/relationship, immutable evidence, memory and opinion/reflection histories, pending intentions/plans and policy metadata. Model files are absent by design. Actual grants/autonomy/role activation must require fresh-body review; reimport/readmit models and verify new measured profile. Keep the original phone intact until continuity comparison passes.

Record APK/source identities, model SHA-256/roles/receipts, Android versions and each pass/failure. Actual second-phone proof, offline ASR availability, two-app Binder authentication and sustained workload/background behavior cannot be inferred from host compilation.
